# RemotePilot relay artwork v1

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, 2026-10-24. These are original
branding assets for the companion, not screenshots of a hardware test.

- `relay-icon-v1.png`: full-resolution square source, reduced to 48×48 for the
  HOME Menu/Homebrew Launcher SMDH.
- `relay-banner-v1.png`: full-resolution 2:1 source, reduced to 256×128 for the
  HOME Menu banner and 200×100 RGB565 for the app's top-screen header.

The build only resizes and converts pixel format. Master art is retained here;
no generated asset is referenced from an external cache location.

## Final icon prompt

Create an original premium app icon for a private homebrew utility called
RemotePilot, used on a Nintendo 3DS HOME Menu. Square composition, no text at all.
A striking sculpted brushed-silver paper-aircraft / flying relay emblem, pointing
diagonally upward-right, with two small stacked rounded screen insets cut into its
center to subtly suggest a dual-screen handheld. One continuous strong recognizable
silhouette that remains readable at 48x48 pixels. Deep midnight navy background
with a restrained teal aurora and one luminous circular navigation ring behind
the emblem. Mint edge light, tiny warm amber accent on the aircraft tip,
sophisticated retro-futurist flight-console aesthetic, refined physical materials
with subtle depth, strong contrast, crisp edges, minimal broad shapes. Edge-to-edge
square dark background, no external border, no rounded app icon mask, no mockup,
no watermark, no Nintendo logos, no controllers or words. Central emblem occupies
about 75 percent of the square, keep all important details within an 8 percent
safe margin. Asset should look like professionally art-directed console homebrew
branding, not a generic stock download arrow.

## Final banner prompt

Create a new wide 2:1 HOME Menu banner for the RemotePilot Homebrew app, using
the immediately preceding silver flying relay emblem and mint navigation ring as
the visual identity reference. Preserve that emblem's distinctive brushed-silver
paper-aircraft silhouette and dual-screen cutouts. Layout for a very small
256x128-pixel console banner: large emblem on the left third, clear typography
on the right two-thirds. Exact prominent text 'RemotePilot' with a smaller line
'HOMEBREW' below. Large clean condensed sans-serif type, off-white silver main word
and mint subtitle, no tiny text. Original polished cinematic console art: midnight
navy, flowing teal aurora, subtle star navigation marks, one warm amber aircraft-tip
accent, restrained holographic route trail, broad high-contrast forms. Professional
and sharp, not a mockup, no watermark, no third-party logos, no Nintendo branding,
no additional words. Keep type and emblem comfortably inside the edges with about
8 percent safe margin. Make the wordmark highly legible after reduction to 256x128.
