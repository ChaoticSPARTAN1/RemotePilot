      SYSTEM SETTINGS
      PSP settings backup
      Not started
      
    
      Copies the PSP's system settings (its theme, date and time, nickname, and network connections with their Wi-Fi passwords) into a new folder in ms0:/BACKUP, then to this computer, and checks every file against what the PSP read. The settings themselves are only read, never changed. Keep these copies private: never attach them to a bug report.
      
    
      
    
        
    
          
    
          PUTTING A BACKUP BACK
          
      
          1. On the PSP, open RemotePilot PSP and press □ Restore.
          2. Pick a backup, then ✕: it checks the copies and shows what will change.
          3. Hold ✕ for two seconds. Each file is written and read back, then the PSP restarts.
          
      
          Only the PSP can restore, with its own buttons: nothing on the network can write your settings. RemotePilot PSP also tells you at start whether your settings still match your newest backup.
